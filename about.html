02

Born and raised

New Bern, North Carolina

Jesse Langley was born July 5th, 1987, in New Bern, North Carolina. Small-town America.

It is the place that gave him his sense of community and the quiet backdrop for his earliest writing.

03

Family

A Marine Corps household

Jesse's late father served at Marine Corps Air Station Cherry Point until his retirement in 1998. Service and discipline were part of the family story from the start.

Jesse has spoken of a good upbringing, one where love and structure went hand in hand.

Values

Hard work. Honesty. Morals. Values.

These were the key ethics instilled in Jesse's upbringing, and they still guide the way he lives and writes.

Language arts

Where the writing began

In high school Jesse was relatively successful in language arts, which is where he says his passion for writing began. He excelled at poetry.

Poetry taught him to say a lot in a few lines, a skill that carries through every lyric he writes.

07

Service

Military route

Jesse's schooling focused heavily on a military route. He attended and completed the four-year NJROTC program.

After his sophomore year he shipped out to Fort Benning, Georgia, as part of his Army National Guard contract as an E-3 (Private First Class), with 92 Yankee, Supply Specialist, as his classification.
