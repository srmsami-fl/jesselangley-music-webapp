My story

Hi, my name is Jesse Langley

I was born on July 5th, 1987, in New Bern, North Carolina. Small-town America, where my late father served at Marine Corps Air Station Cherry Point until his retirement in 1998.

I had a good upbringing. Hard work, honesty, morals and values were all key ethics instilled in us. In high school I found my passion for writing, and my first song was fully written in 2002.

06

Service

NJROTC and the Army National Guard

Jesse's schooling focused heavily on a military route. He completed the four-year NJROTC program, and after his sophomore year he shipped out to Fort Benning, Georgia, as part of his Army National Guard contract.

He served as an E-3 (Private First Class) with 92 Yankee, Supply Specialist, as his classification, then returned to graduate high school and complete a four-year contract with the Army.
