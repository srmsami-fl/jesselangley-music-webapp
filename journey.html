The journey

School, service, work

The jobs and the discipline behind the songs.

4Years of NJROTC
4Year Army contract
2Years to lead installer
9Years in HVAC
01

Service

NJROTC

Jesse's schooling focused heavily on a military route. He attended and completed the four-year NJROTC program.

It built structure and discipline that would serve him through every job that followed.

02

Basic Training

Fort Benning, Georgia

After his sophomore year, Jesse shipped out to Fort Benning, Georgia, as part of his Army National Guard contract, to attend Basic Training.

E-3 Private First Class92 YankeeSupply Specialist

Duty completed

He returned home, graduated high school, and completed his four-year contract with the Army.
03

Career, part one

The fast food years

Throughout his late teens to mid 20s, Jesse specialized in the fast food industry.

He earned ServSafe certifications for multiple well-known companies, and held a couple of assistant manager positions in the latter years.

04

Pivot

At 29, a new direction

At 29, Jesse transitioned out of the fast food industry to focus on a career development pathway. That is when he found, and fell in love with, HVAC.

It was a fresh start, and he took it seriously.

05

Career, part two

Working his way up

Jesse started at bare minimum entry-level pay and worked his way diligently through the ranks, becoming a lead installer within two years.

06

Family at work

A big family

Jesse loved everyone there, from the managers to his co-workers. It genuinely felt like a big family to him.

Nine years

Nine years in HVAC, until a career-ending stroke.
